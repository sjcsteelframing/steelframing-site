Principle

The market comes before the building system

Demand, location, product, achievable price, operation and legal classification determine whether a project has the economic basis to move forward. Only then does it make sense to ask which building system best meets the project’s goals and constraints.

Light Steel Framing can change timeline, logistics, standardization and cost predictability. These effects depend on design, crew, suppliers and actual execution conditions.

The building system does not fix a bad investment.
