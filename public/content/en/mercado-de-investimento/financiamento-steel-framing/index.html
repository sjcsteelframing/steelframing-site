01 · Planning

The exit strategy starts before construction

Those who build to sell should not leave the possibility of buyer financing to the end of the project. A development can be technically well executed and still face commercial difficulty if the property is not registered, if the documentation is not organized, or if the asking price is far from the value recognized by the market.

The most useful question is not just “Can Steel Framing be financed?”.
It is: “Is this specific property being designed, built and documented to participate in a real estate credit transaction when it is finished?”

This shift in perspective turns financeability into a planning variable for the investment.

02 · Framework

A Light Steel Framing property can participate in a real estate financing transaction

Yes. The existence of Light Steel Framing does not, by itself, prevent a property from being accepted in a real estate financing transaction. CAIXA includes ABNT NBR 16970:2022 — Light Steel Framing among examples of construction systems with an ABNT standard in its housing guide for individual construction.

This does not mean that any construction called “Steel Frame” is automatically accepted. Qualification depends on the solution being effectively covered by the applicable standards and on the property meeting the other technical, legal, registration and economic conditions of the transaction.

Technical characteristicSpecific standard applicable to the system.
Practical consequenceReduces uncertainty about minimum design and performance criteria.
Possible economic consequenceMay simplify technical qualification within the conditions covered by the standard.
03 · Operation

Financing construction is different from financing the purchase of a finished property

Credit to build

The credit is contracted by whoever will carry out the construction. Depending on the modality, the institution may analyze design, budget, physical-financial schedule, land, technical responsibility, financial capacity and viability of the operation.

Buyer credit to acquire the finished property

The flow is different: land → design → construction → registration → finished property → buyer → credit analysis → property appraisal → contracting → registration → payment to seller.

In this case, the bank is not financing the construction for the investor. It is extending credit to the buyer with the property as collateral for the transaction.

04 · Technical standard

The role of ABNT NBR 16970

The ABNT NBR 16970:2022 series is the Brazilian normative reference specifically for construction systems in Light Steel Framing structured with cold-formed light steel profiles with thin-sheet enclosures.

  • Part 1 — Performance;
  • Part 2 — Structural design;
  • Part 3 — Interfaces between systems.

Part 1 applies to residential buildings — single-family or multi-family — and non-residential buildings of up to two floors. This limit defines the scope of application of that part of the standard; it does not mean that larger LSF buildings are technically impossible.

05 · Technical assessment

When SiNAT, DATec and FAD come in

The SiNAT — National System of Technical Assessments integrates PBQP-H and currently evaluates both innovative products and conventional systems. In the current system, innovative products or systems may result in a DATec; conventional systems evaluated by SiNAT may result in a FAD.

Important: “more than two floors” should not be turned in isolation into a rule such as “always requires a DATec”. The situation falls outside the scope of NBR 16970-1 and must be analyzed according to the adopted solution, the technical documentation and the requirements of the institution.
06 · Credit

What the financial institution really analyzes

In a financed acquisition of a finished property there are at least three fronts: buyer, property and transaction. The construction system enters within the property analysis; it does not replace the income, regularity, value, collateral or conditions analysis of the chosen credit line.

07 · Traceability

Design, technical responsibility and construction documentation

An LSF construction requires legally qualified professionals within their responsibilities. Where applicable, there must be architectural design, structural design, complementary designs, technical responsibility for design and execution, and documentation of changes made during construction.

In LSF, a large part of the system is concealed after enclosures are closed. For this reason, steelframing.com.br recommends that construction documentation be built during execution.

08 · Best practice

Technical Property Dossier

The dossier can gather designs, technical responsibilities, descriptive memorandum, specifications, photographic records before enclosures, location of reinforcements, documentation of critical interfaces, operation and maintenance manual, and completion documents.

Technical characteristicOrganized traceability of what was designed and executed.
Practical consequenceLess uncertainty in maintenance, renovation and technical assistance.
Possible economic consequenceLower risk of destructive interventions and better documentary preservation of the asset.
09 · Value

How bank appraisal works

At CAIXA, the property appraisal is carried out by an accredited engineer to verify whether the asset meets the qualification conditions and can serve as collateral for the financing. The appraisal of urban properties is governed by the ABNT NBR 14653 series.

There is insufficient basis to claim that appraisers “typically undervalue Steel Framing due to unfamiliarity.” An appraisal may diverge from the asking price, but this must be treated as a market and methodology issue.

10 · Market

Construction cost, selling price and appraisal value are not the same thing

When the appraisal is below the negotiated price

At CAIXA, the value used in the transaction is the lower of the negotiated price and the appraised value. If the appraisal is lower, this may require greater contribution of own resources by the buyer, renegotiation of the price, or revision of the commercial strategy.

11 · Financial structure

Own capital and production financing

With own capital, the investor finances the development with own or partner resources. This eliminates the interest on a specific production debt, but does not eliminate the opportunity cost of the capital employed.

Production financing adds costs, controls, measurements and conditions, but may allow the investor to not concentrate all the necessary capital in the development. Suitability depends on the purpose, scale, risk profile and conditions of the transaction.

12 · Time and capital

Timeline, cost of capital and liquidity

In LSF, a well-coordinated chain between design, manufacturing, logistics and assembly can reduce timelines and improvisation. The possible economic consequence is less time with capital tied up, less exposure to production interest, and earlier registration and sale. None of this is automatic.

15 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can a Steel Framing house be financed by CAIXA?

It can participate in a financing transaction, provided the property and the construction solution meet the applicable qualification conditions.

Does all Steel Framing eliminate the need for a DATec?

No. The existence of NBR 16970 does not automatically cover any solution or building. Projects outside the scope of application need to have their technical qualification verified.

Does the bank pay the price the seller asked for?

Not necessarily. At CAIXA, the value used in the transaction is the lower of the negotiated price and the appraised value.

Does building with own resources eliminate financial cost?

It eliminates the interest on a specific production debt, but not the opportunity cost of the capital employed.

Does Steel Framing guarantee faster construction and higher ROI?

No. The system changes variables that can influence timeline and return. The result depends on design, team, supplies, logistics, market, cost of capital and sales strategy.

16 · Conclusion

Financeability must be part of the project planning

What really matters is building an asset that, at the end, is technically defined + correctly executed + documented + registered + aligned with the market + suitable for analysis by the financial institution.

LSF does not automatically generate financeability, liquidity or return. It changes the way the building is designed and executed and, when well coordinated, can change timeline, predictability and use of capital.

The financing strategy for the exit must start before construction.
