Investment Market · Studio units

Studio units for rental income

Lease or boarding house, taxes, permits and design: what weighs on the decision before building a studio-unit building in Brazil — and where Light Steel Framing fits in.

Starting point

The business model comes before the design

A building of studio units — kitnets, as they are called in Brazil — looks like a simple investment: land, construction and monthly rent. But the same floor plan can be run as a residential lease or as lodging, and each path has its own law, taxes, permits and costs.

That choice even changes the design. In a boarding house, at least one unit must be accessible (5% of rooms) and the fire-safety class changes. In a residential building of studios up to 35 m², accessibility is required in common areas, not inside the units. That is why it has to be made before the design goes to the city for approval.

The business model drives the design, not the other way around.

Building system

Where Light Steel Framing fits in

The building system does not define the tax model or guarantee returns. In buildings with repeated units, panel modulation, early coordination of building services and planned assembly can standardize execution and make cost and timeline more predictable — provided there is a proper design, a trained crew and coordinated supply.
