Starting point

Steel is the structure. The building is the complete system.

Light Steel Framing (LSF) is primarily structured with cold-formed light galvanized steel profiles, organized to form panels, floors, roofs, and other elements capable of receiving and transferring loads.

But defining LSF simply as a "structure made of steel profiles" is incomplete. Cladding, insulation, membranes, finishes, mechanical systems, foundations, connections, and all other subsystems must work in an integrated manner.

The building's performance is the result of the complete system.
A simple definition

Light + Steel + Framing

Light refers to the lightweight structure compared to higher-mass structural systems. Steel means steel. Framing relates to forming a skeleton composed of connected elements.

The term does not simply mean building a metal wall: it means conceiving a building from a light steel structure and subsystems designed to work together.

Brazil

ABNT NBR 16970

ABNT NBR 16970, published in 2022, covers building systems structured with cold-formed light steel profiles, with thin-sheet cladding.

It is organized in three parts: performance, structural design, and interfaces between systems.

Normative compliance does not replace design: application, scope limits, and other technical references must be verified for each project.

Summary

It's not just about switching materials

In systems with a higher degree of industrialization, a significant portion of decisions must be made in advance, because structure, components, mechanical systems, and interfaces must arrive at execution with a higher level of definition.

Light Steel Framing is an integrated construction logic — design, preparation, assembly, and performance working as one.
