OUR ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK

A technical advantage only matters when we understand its consequence.

The construction system changes how a project is designed, fabricated, transported and executed. These changes can affect schedule, cost, risk, performance and operations — but the outcome depends on the real conditions of each project.

  1. 01
    Technical characteristicWhat changes in the system.
  2. 02
    Practical consequenceWhat changes in design and execution.
  3. 03
    Economic consequenceWhat changes in schedule, CAPEX, OPEX, revenue or risk.

PERFORMANCE

The building is a complete system.

A Light Steel Framing structure, in isolation, does not determine energy efficiency, acoustic performance, fire resistance, durability or sustainability.

These results depend on the composition of walls and roofs, materials, detailing, execution, interfaces and exposure conditions.

Go deeper into the system

INVESTMENT MARKET

Steel Framing doesn't generate ROI automatically.

The construction system changes variables that participate in economic outcomes: CAPEX, schedule, opportunity cost, OPEX, revenue start date, risk, scale and predictability.

The right analysis starts with the project — and only then asks which construction system best meets its goals and constraints.
